### Question 2 (10 points)
**Question:** Explain the concept of "echo chambers" as described in the passage. How do social media algorithms contribute to this phenomenon, and what are the potential consequences?

**Your Answer:**
Echo chambers are when people only see information that agrees with what they already believe. The passage says social media algorithms make this worse because they show you content based on what you've liked before, so you keep seeing the same types of posts and opinions. The algorithms care more about keeping you engaged than showing you accurate or different viewpoints. This causes problems like polarization, which means people get more extreme in their views, and they don't get exposed to different perspectives. The passage also says this can spread misinformation because sensational content gets amplified. It's basically like being in a room where everyone agrees with you and you never hear other sides of an argument.

### Question 3 (10 points)
**Question:** The passage mentions that technology has "blurred the boundaries between work and personal life." Discuss what this means and explain the negative effects this can have on individuals.

**Your Answer:**
Blurred boundaries means that work and personal time are mixed together now because of mobile devices. Before, when you left the office, you were done with work. But now your boss can email or message you at any time, even on weekends or at night. The passage says this causes increased stress and burnout. I think this happens because people can never really relax or disconnect from work. You might be having dinner with your family but still checking work emails. This constant availability makes it hard to have a proper work-life balance and can affect your mental health and relationships with family and friends.

**Question:** The author suggests that the key question is "how to harness technology's benefits while mitigating its negative effects." Based on the passage and your own understanding, propose two specific strategies that individuals or society could implement to achieve this balance.

**Your Answer:**
First strategy: Schools should teach digital literacy as a required subject. The passage mentions developing digital literacy skills as important. Students need to learn how to identify misinformation, understand how algorithms work, and use technology responsibly. This would help people make better choices about their technology use and not fall for fake news or get trapped in echo chambers.

Second strategy: Companies should create "right to disconnect" policies where employees cannot be contacted outside of work hours except for real emergencies. This would help with the blurred boundaries problem the passage talks about. If there are clear rules about when people can be reached for work, it would reduce stress and burnout and help people maintain better work-life balance.
